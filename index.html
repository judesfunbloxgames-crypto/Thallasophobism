Abyssal Breach 3D: Subcomplex 115

You are inside a flooded deep-sea submarine complex. Traverse 115 connected rooms in first person.

Each room can spawn entities with different weaknesses:

  • Brine Stalker: reacts to sound (crouch to reduce noise).
  • Lumen Leech: hunts your light (toggle lamp off with L).
  • Sonar Widow: tracks line-of-sight (hide by lockers with E).
  • Anchor Hound: can be distracted by flares (F).

If integrity or sanity reaches zero, you are lost to the deep.
